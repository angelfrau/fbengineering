About Us

Frau & Bär Engineering es un estudio de ingeniería industrial fundado en Mallorca con un único objetivo: entregar proyectos de la máxima calidad con un servicio eficiente, cercano y de alto valor.

We are Murti Bär and Àngel Frau, industrial engineers. We combine technical rigour, attention to detail and a clear vision for the future: the digitalisation of industry.

Our way of working is straightforward yet demanding: we listen to the client, design tailored solutions, and execute them with the best available technology.
